# Bright-TV-Case-Study

## Project Overview

This project analyzes streaming platform data for **BrightTV** to uncover insights into **user behaviour, content performance, viewing patterns, and business growth opportunities**.

The goal was to transform raw streaming data into meaningful insights that can help BrightTV better understand its users, identify high-performing content, and make data-driven business decisions.

## Objectives

The main objectives of this project were to:

* Analyze user behaviour and viewing activity.
* Identify high-performing content.
* Understand viewing patterns and engagement.
* Identify trends within the streaming data.
* Find opportunities to improve user engagement and business growth.
* Provide data-driven recommendations.

## Tools Used

## Project Workflow

### 1. Data Exploration

The dataset was explored to understand its structure, variables, and available information.

### 2. Data Cleaning

SQL was used to clean and prepare the data for analysis.

### 3. Data Analysis

The data was analyzed to identify patterns in:

* User activity
* Viewing behaviour

* Watch time

### 4. Visualization

Excel pivot tables and charts were used to present the findings and make key trends easier to understand.

### 5. Insights & Recommendations

The results were interpreted to identify opportunities for improving engagement, content performance, and overall business growth.

## Key Insights

* The analysis covered **9,995 viewing sessions** from **4,386 users**, totalling **1,522 hours** across **19 channels**.
* Viewing peaked in **March**, which generated **55.1% of total watch time**. **30 March 2016** was the busiest day, with **86.5 hours** of viewing.
* **Saturday** recorded the highest viewing, while **Monday** recorded the lowest. Weekend days averaged more watch time than weekdays.
* Sports content dominated viewing. **ICC Cricket World Cup 2011** was the leading content title, accounting for **27.1% of total watch time**, while the **top three channels generated 60.3%** of total watch time.
* The **afternoon** was the most popular viewing period, accounting for **44.3% of total watch time**.
* **Gauteng and Western Cape** contributed **56.1% of all viewing**.
* Viewers aged **25–44** were the core audience, accounting for **70.2% of total watch time**.
